What it does

  • Turns the car's one-minute files into one video per event, with every camera in sync and the trigger marked.
  • Imports straight from the car's USB drive, skipping what you already have, and can clear the drive afterwards.
  • Shows where each event happened on a map, and patterns by month, hour, weekday and place.
  • Trims ten-minute Sentry recordings down to the moments that matter, and can clean up Sentry events at home.
  • Makes one merged video of all cameras to share, and exports any range from any camera.
  • Everything stays on your computer. No account, no cloud, no telemetry.

Getting started

  1. Run the installer. Windows shows a "Windows protected your PC" warning because the app is not code-signed: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. In the car, turn off Controls → Safety → Encrypt Dashcam Recordings if your car has it. Encrypted clips can only be played in the car or on Tesla's website.
  3. When the app first opens, start a new library, or choose the folder where you already keep your clips. Nothing is copied or moved for that.
  4. Plug the car's USB drive into your computer. The app offers to import whatever on it is new; put the drive back in the car when it finishes.

Two ways to run it

Desktop app

Install it on your PC and import from the USB drive whenever you like. Nothing else to set up.

Home server

Run it with Docker on a NAS or home server and open it from any browser, phone included. Pairs with teslausb to upload clips by itself when the car gets home. How to.
